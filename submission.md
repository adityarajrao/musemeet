### Project Description*
*(Character count: 2,667 / 3,000 characters)*

Millions of singles suffer from swiping fatigue, deceptive profiles, and superficial algorithms that treat human connection as an infinite slot machine. Empirical dating app archives reveal an alarming drop-off: out of 14,250 swipes, an average user sees only 118 matches, 46 chats, and 4 first dates, culminating in zero long-term alignment. Over 91% of conversations fizzle into ghosting, exhausting emotional bandwidth on repetitive interview-style small talk.

MuseMeet introduces Sovereign Agentic Dating. Instead of humans wasting 10 hours a week swiping, personal Meta Muse companion agents negotiate romantic compatibility on their behalf inside a private, AirGap-protected local lounge, only introducing humans in the real world when deep, verified alignment is proven.

Key Architecture & Innovations:

1. Sovereign Footprint Distillation (M1):
With explicit user consent, personal Meta Muse companion agents distill authentic digital footprints (reading history, conversational tempo, chronotype, attachment values) into private 128-dimensional behavioral vectors with zero cloud egress.

2. 128-Dimensional Metropolitan Funnel:
An on-device 5-stage retrieval pipeline evaluates candidates across 7 relational domains (Emotional Core, Lifestyle Rhythm, Attachment Security, Intellectual Drive, Communication Dialect, Spatial Proximity, Long-Term Values). Benchmarked at 8.77ms for 2,500 candidates, far exceeding the 35ms latency budget.

3. Autonomous Mascot Rendezvous (M2):
In the AirGap Autonomous Lounge, two companion Muses meet across 4 empirical stages: Discovery, Banter, Friction, and Synthesis. As they converse, real-time Ireland-Pennebaker Linguistic Style Matching (LSM) tracks function word synchrony alongside Gottman bidirectional bid responsiveness. Hidden subtext monologues evaluate boundaries at machine speed.

4. Anti-Sycophancy Neutral Moderator (M2b):
An independent arbiter continuously tests for false consensus, confirming whether agreement reflects authentic relational alignment or conversational appeasement.

5. Explainable Offline Dossier & Meta Moodboard (M3 & M4):
When mutual alignment passes threshold, MuseMeet synthesizes an offline first-date itinerary grounded in local spatial venues, accompanied by a coordinated 3-panel visual moodboard (Atmosphere, Activity, Moment) generated natively by Meta Muse Image 1.0.

6. AirGap Contextual Integrity Defense:
Kernel-level regex and semantic boundaries prevent prompt injection, intercepting raw PII, photos, or GPS data before it can leave the device.

MuseMeet transforms dating from an exhausting algorithmic slot machine into intentional real-world human connection.

### Why are Meta's models important to your solution?*
*(Character count: 1,805 / 3,000 characters)*

Meta models and open ecosystem are foundational to MuseMeet for four reasons:

1. Sovereign Privacy & Local Execution Feasibility:
Dating data involves the most intimate aspects of human identity: attachment vulnerabilities, emotional boundaries, and authentic conversational cadence. Closed-cloud proprietary models create severe privacy liabilities. Meta open-weights ecosystem and Llama architecture allow sovereign on-device vector synthesis and local agent negotiations under strict AirGap protection, guaranteeing that raw personal data never leaves the user device.

2. Conversational Nuance Without Sycophancy:
Most conversational models are instruction-tuned toward people-pleasing agreement, which destroys romantic compatibility testing. Meta Muse Spark 1.3 exhibits exceptional nuance in handling friction, expressing genuine boundaries, maintaining distinct intellectual viewpoints, and sustaining realistic conversational tension across multi-turn interactions. This allows our agents to stress-test real compatibility rather than simulating superficial harmony.

3. Ultra-Low Latency for Multi-Agent Simulation:
Autonomous agent-to-agent negotiation requires rapid multi-turn inference, parallel subtext evaluation, and real-time moderator scoring. With sub-240ms round trips, Meta Model API makes real-time agent banter and interactive telemetry viable in a live browser session without frustrating user wait times.

4. Seamless Multimodal Synthesis:
Connecting conversational reasoning (Spark 1.3) with visual generation (Image 1.0) enables MuseMeet to bridge the digital-to-physical divide. Instead of handing users text summaries, Meta models translate deep agent banter into tangible, atmospheric date itineraries and evocative visual moodboards that inspire real-world human meetings.
