## 🌿 Core Invariants & Architectural Boundaries

1. **The Human-Agency Non-Interference Guarantee:**  
   The platform never coaches, scripts, or provides "pre-mortems" for how humans interact on real dates. Muse agents operate purely as background discovery and compatibility verifiers. Once an introduction occurs, the AI steps back completely.
2. **Sovereign AirGap Zero-Leakage Privacy:**  
   Raw photos, video frames, social media histories, and financial transaction logs **physically never leave the user's local client**. All vision and heuristic inference executes locally (simulating Meta Llama 3.2 Vision / SAM 3.1). Only high-level, non-inversible mathematical vectors enter the digital lounge.
3. **Continuous Manifold Over Categorical Profiling:**  
   No crude categorical drop-downs (no discrete racial classifications or net-worth brackets). All dimensions are continuous vectors (0.0 to 1.0 or unit L2 spheres) capturing aesthetic vibes, somatic pacing, and lifestyle rhythms.

### MODULE A: Sovereign Aesthetic & Dwell-Time Intelligence Protocol

#### Task A1: On-Device Reel Dwell-Time & APV Extractor (`agent_engine/vault/reel_profiler.js`)
* **Priority:** CRITICAL (First-in-class implicit aesthetic modeling) | **Estimated Time:** 2 hours
* **Target File:** `musemeet/agent_engine/vault/reel_profiler.js`
* **Test File:** `musemeet/tests/test_reel_profiler.js`
* **Mathematical Specification:**
  * Involuntary attention reveals authentic attraction far more accurately than stated preferences.
  * For each consumed media event $i$, compute an attention weight $w_i$:
    $$w_i = \min\left(3.0, \; 1.0 + 0.5 \cdot \log_2(1 + \text{rewatch\_count}_i) + \frac{\max(0, \text{dwell\_ms}_i - \text{baseline\_ms})}{2000}\right)$$
  * The local vision model extracts a 32-dim latent embedding $v_i$ for human subjects in high-attention frames ($w_i \ge 1.3$).
  * The aggregate **Aesthetic Preference Vector (APV)** is computed via weighted centroid and normalized to unit L2 length:
    $$\mathbf{APV} = \text{normalizeL2}\left( \sum_{i=1}^M w_i \cdot \mathbf{v}_i \right)$$
* **Requirements & Guardrails:**
  1. Strict opt-in check: If `consent_given !== true`, immediately throw `SecurityError`.
  2. AirGap Guard: Ensure no raw URLs, keyframe image buffers, or timestamps are exported in the return object—only `user_id`, `sample_count`, `apv_vector` (32 floats), and `confidence_score`.
  3. Provide deterministic fallback fixtures for testing (`usr_elena_01`, `usr_julian_01`).

#### Task A2: Bilateral Satisficing Gate Integration (`agent_engine/router/candidate_funnel.js`)
* **Priority:** HIGH | **Estimated Time:** 1 hour
* **Target File:** `musemeet/agent_engine/router/candidate_funnel.js`
* **Specification:**
  * Grounded in Hunt, Eastwick & Finkel (2015): Physical attraction functions as a non-linear satisficing threshold gate ($\theta = 0.58$), not an infinite linear optimizer.
  * Implement `evaluateBilateralAestheticGate(candidateA, candidateB, threshold = 0.58)`:
    $$\text{Sim}_{A \to B} = \cos(\mathbf{APV}_A, \mathbf{VPT}_B), \quad \text{Sim}_{B \to A} = \cos(\mathbf{APV}_B, \mathbf{VPT}_A)$$
    $$\text{Passes} \iff \text{Sim}_{A \to B} \ge \theta_A \land \text{Sim}_{B \to A} \ge \theta_B$$
  * If the bilateral gate fails, the candidate is disqualified from the Top 3 finalist shortlist before expensive LLM lounge dialogues execute.
