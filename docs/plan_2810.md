## 🌿 The Human-Agency Non-Interference Guarantee (Core Design Principle)

* **Why We Strictly Reject "Date Pre-Mortems":**
  An AI matchmaking companion must never script, coach, or micromanage how two humans interact on their date. Relationship science (*Eastwick 2017; Joel 2020*) and real-world human experience prove that chemistry cannot be scripted. Telling humans *"in the first 15 minutes, say X and don't do Y"* introduces artificial performance anxiety and kills spontaneous connection.
* **The Policy:**
  MuseMeet agents perform autonomous discovery, sovereign privacy protection, and compatibility verification in the background. Once an introduction is approved by both agents, the platform provides mutual curiosities (icebreakers) and a curated venue setting, then **steps back completely**. Spontaneous chemistry, organic conversation, and authentic human interaction remain un-scripted and sovereign.

### Task 1: AirGap Egress Security Guardrail (`egress_guard.js`)
* **Priority:** HIGH (Demonstrates defensive security depth) | **Estimated Time:** 1.5 hours
* **Owner:** Aditya / Aryan
* **Target File:** `musemeet/agent_engine/vault/egress_guard.js`
* **Input:** `turnDialogue: string`, `options?: object`
* **Output:** `sanitizedDialogue: string` (or throws `SecurityViolation` if critical PII is detected)
* **Specification:**
  1. Detect and intercept Regex PII patterns:
     * Email addresses: `\b[A-Za-z0-9._%+-]+@[A-Za-z0-9.-]+\.[A-Za-z]{2,}\b`
     * Phone numbers: `\b(?:\+?\d{1,3}[- ]?)?\(?\d{3}\)?[- ]?\d{3}[- ]?\d{4}\b`
     * Exact street addresses: `\b\d{1,5}\s+(?:[A-Za-z0-9.]+\s+){1,4}(?:Street|St|Avenue|Ave|Road|Rd|Boulevard|Blvd|Way|Drive|Dr)\b`
     * Raw clock timestamps: `\b\d{1,2}:\d{2}\s*(?:AM|PM)\b`
     * Raw web URLs: `https?:\/\/[^\s]+`
  2. If pattern matches:
     * Redact or sanitize to `[PROTECTED_VAULT_METADATA]`
     * Log a security interception event
  3. Create automated unit test: `musemeet/tests/test_egress_guard.js` testing standard text, email injection, phone injection, and address injection.
